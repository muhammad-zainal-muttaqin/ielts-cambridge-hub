Test format

Test format and how to record results
  1. Click the source with the blue border (the main source). The test opens in a new tab. The Timer button starts a countdown for the length of the module.
  2. Listening and Reading: enter your correct answers out of 40 and the band is calculated for you. Writing and Speaking: pick your self-assessed band. Filling in either one marks the test as done.
  3. Set a target band in the skill summary. The skill with the largest gap gets the Priority label.
  4. Use notes for question types you often get wrong. The search box also searches your notes.
